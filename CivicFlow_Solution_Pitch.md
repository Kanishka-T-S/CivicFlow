# CivicFlow – AI-Powered Public Grievance Automation System

### Problem
Citizens often struggle to report public issues such as damaged roads, water shortages, poor sanitation, and waste management problems through traditional complaint systems.

### Solution
CivicFlow is an AI-powered, voice-first public grievance platform that simplifies civic complaint reporting using Gnani AI speech technology.

### How It Works
1. Citizens submit complaints through voice input.
2. Gnani AI converts speech into text.
3. AI identifies the issue category.
4. The system collects relevant location details.
5. Citizens review and confirm structured complaints.

### Key Features
- Multilingual voice-based complaint reporting.
- Gnani AI speech-to-text integration.
- Automated complaint categorization.
- Location-based complaint information.
- Simplified complaint submission workflow.

### Technology Stack
Python, Flask, Gnani AI API, HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

### Target Users
Citizens, local communities and public service administrators.

### Innovation and Impact
CivicFlow aims to make civic complaint reporting more accessible and inclusive by reducing manual effort and supporting voice-based interaction. It has the potential to improve communication between citizens and public service departments.

**Project Status:** Working prototype with future scope for integration with official government grievance platforms.
